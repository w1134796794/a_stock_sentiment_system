# Strategy factor experiment: 20260601-20260720

## 1. Purpose and constraints

This experiment tests whether strategy-specific factor combinations can improve the
short-term simulation result without using future data. The requested target was a
return above 20% for June and July 2026. That target is treated as an evaluation
goal, not as a parameter-fitting constraint. A combination is not published merely
because it reaches a target on a small historical slice.

The experiment follows these constraints:

- Training data ends at `20260531`.
- Evaluation uses `20260601` through `20260720` only.
- Only candidates generated by the corresponding strategy are used.
- Entry requires the actual next-day minute signal and is filled at the next minute
  open. A sealed board without a fill is not counted as a trade.
- Exit, fees, risk controls, position limits, and market gates use the production
  backtest engine.
- Candidate factors, entry rules, and exit rules are not tuned on the evaluation
  trades after seeing their profit and loss.

## 3. Strategy-specific evidence

Historical confirmed-entry samples through May 2026 show that the three strategies
do not share the same factor structure:

### First-board launch

The strongest training features were board height, behavior repair, crowding decay,
seal time, behavior acceleration, sector LHB resonance, attention, float-market-cap
fit, sector rotation, and seal quality. Strong continuation had positive historical
returns, but only 11 training samples; continuation-only selection produced no valid
out-of-sample fills. It is useful as an enhancement, not yet as a hard mandatory
condition.

### Mainline leader

The stronger features were sector heat, seal time, crowding penalty, institution
consensus, sector LHB resonance, capital-flow consensus, intraday seal quality,
attention, sector rotation, and mainline persistence. High-open acceleration had a
negative training mean and a materially worse downside tail, so it was removed from
the allowed entry modes.

### Weak-to-strong

The stronger features were sector resonance, technical score, seal time, mainline
persistence, behavior repair, liquidity, float-market-cap fit, sector rotation, and
board position. The strategy remains minute-confirmation only; a low or flat open by
itself is not an entry signal.

## 4. Out-of-sample results

The experiment trains ExtraTrees regressors on confirmed-entry forward returns and
ranks only later strategy candidates. The best results found under the fixed
out-of-sample contract were:

| Variant | Selection | Return | Max drawdown | Win rate | Profit factor | Closed trades |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Model ranking | Top 1 per strategy/day | +1.68% | -1.00% | 66.7% | 1.30 | 6 |
| Focus rules | Top 1 per strategy/day | +1.72% | -0.96% | 60.0% | 2.73 | 5 |
| Model ranking | Top 2 per strategy/day | **+2.43%** | -1.97% | 60.0% | 1.43 | 10 |
| Focus rules | Top 2 per strategy/day | +1.19% | -0.96% | 50.0% | 2.09 | 6 |
| First-board continuation only | Top 1 | 0.00% | 0.00% | - | - | 0 |

The best honest result is `+2.43%`, not 20%. Reaching 20% over roughly 35 trading
days with only 5-10 closed trades would require extreme concentration, hindsight
thresholds, or materially different leverage. Such a result would not establish a
repeatable strategy.

## 5. Production configuration changes

The following conservative changes were applied to
`config/strategy_combinations.yaml`:

- First-board launch now uses the `near_limit` training universe and its own
  `first_board_launch` weight profile.
- Mainline leader now uses its own `mainline_leader` weight profile instead of the
  shared `default` artifact.
- First-board, mainline leader, and weak-to-strong each cap simultaneous positions at
  one for the current small-capital product preset.
- Mainline leader no longer permits `acceleration` entry until that mode has positive
  rolling out-of-sample evidence.
- Manual fallback priors were rebalanced toward the factors supported by the
  strategy-specific training evidence.
- Small-sample thresholds from the exploratory rule search were not promoted.

The screening pipeline now retries transient DuckDB lock conflicts. If any selected
strategy fails, the whole screening date fails instead of silently publishing a
partial strategy comparison.

## 6. Production-chain validation

After regenerating all three strategy outputs for every trading day in the test
window, the normal backtest controller was run with the product configuration:

- initial capital: 100,000
- entry mode: hybrid minute confirmation
- sizing: fixed risk
- exit policy: strategy specific
- enabled strategies: first-board launch, mainline leader, and weak-to-strong
- costs: commission, stamp duty, and 0.2% slippage

The audited batch is `20260721_005539`:

| Metric | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Loaded candidates | 190 |
| Confirmed entries | 2 |
| Closed trades | 2 |
| Win rate | 50.0% |
| Total return | **-0.43%** |
| Maximum drawdown | -0.85% |
| Profit/loss ratio | 0.21 |

This result is better than the earlier page result near `-1.94%`, but it is still
not a profitable production strategy. The two closed trades were weak-to-strong
Yidun Electronics (`+1.31%`) and first-board Tianfeng Securities (`-5.57%`).

The main conversion losses were not ranking-factor failures alone:

| Funnel reason | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Weak-market rejection | 91 |
| Market-state score rejection | 58 |
| Broke opening low | 13 |
| Missing sector confirmation | 9 |
| Missing auction evidence | 10 |

The model-ranking experiment and the production backtest therefore measure
different things. The research ranking selected up to two predicted opportunities
per strategy/day and returned `+2.43%`; the product chain retained its market gates,
data-completeness rules, strategy ranking, and risk sizing and returned `-0.43%`.
The experiment model must not be promoted until it passes the gate below with more
closed trades and the missing auction/sector evidence is available.

## 8. Promotion gate

A factor combination should enter the default product configuration only after it
meets all of these conditions:

1. At least 20 closed out-of-sample trades for that strategy and entry mode.
2. Positive out-of-sample profit in at least two separate months.
3. Profit factor above 1.30 after fees and realistic minute fills.
4. Maximum drawdown remains within the selected small-capital risk budget.
5. No single stock, day, or market regime explains most of the profit.
6. The same candidate-to-entry-to-backtest chain is used in the page and experiment.

The next useful improvement is more recent out-of-sample coverage, especially valid
continuation entries for first-board launch. More parameter search on the current ten
trades would mostly optimize noise.
